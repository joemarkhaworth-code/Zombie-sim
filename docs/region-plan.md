# The region: New England, early 1980s (plan, for approval)

Map: [region-plan.png](region-plan.png) (drawn by region-plan-map.py), on the real 25 x 25 screens (179 x 279 tiles).
Replaces the Brackenby town plan (town-plan.md), which was realistic but not fun: one continuous town at even
density, no arrivals, no landmarks, no reason for routes.

## Design rules (Pokémon / Zelda / Stardew)

- On the grid, like the movement: roads, trails, the railway and the river only run north-south or east-west,
  with square corners. Places, yards, fields, the lake and the mountains have blocky edges. No diagonals.

- A few compact places, each small enough to learn by heart (2x2 to 5x4 screens), with a strong identity and
  one or two landmarks to steer by. Arriving somewhere is an event.
- Wilderness between them: forest, the lake, the mountains, the river. Routes are designed: they wind, branch and
  dead-end on purpose, with chokepoints (the bridges, the trestle, the notch) and secrets off the path.
- Places you can see but can't reach yet, opened by key items (step 20): the island (a boat), the logging camp
  (bolt cutters), the old mine (flashlight), the river shallows (waders), the notch (blocked by a rockslide).
- Danger and loot fall with distance from Haverly. The wilderness is quieter but exposed at night, so where you
  set up your safe house, and whether you get back before dark, is a real choice.

## The places (names provisional)

1. **Haverly**, the town (about 5 x 4 screens): Main Street, the town green with the white church, the diner,
   general store, hardware store, bank, post office, police and fire station, movie theater, the train depot.
2. **Carrick Mills**, the mill town on the falls: brick mills, the dam, workers' row houses, the covered bridge.
3. **Loon Lake**, the lake village under the mountains: summer cabins, the lodge, boathouse and marina.
4. **Apple Hill**, the farm village: farms, barns, orchards, the feed store, the grange hall. You start at home here.
5. **Cooper's Corner**, the crossroads on Route 9: gas station, motel, bar, the drive-in.
6. **Spruce Hollow**, the logging town up north: sawmill, lumber yard, bunkhouse, company store, the gun shop.
   The logging camp is beyond its padlocked gate.
7. **Ledgeview**, the quarry village in the south: granite sheds, the Odd Fellows hall, the school, the church.

## Finds in the woods (about a screen each, on trails)

A hunting camp (three cabins), the hermit's cabin, a ranger cabin on the lake shore, a sugar shack, a trapper's
cabin, a cabin colony in the pines, the town dump, a campground left in a hurry, a radio tower, a hilltop cemetery,
an abandoned farmhouse, a crashed light aircraft, a fishing shack on Mud Pond, a boarded-up roadside motel.

## Between them

- **The Kennock River** from Loon Lake past Haverly, through a gorge, over the falls at Carrick Mills.
- **The railway** across the region; a passenger train is stopped on the trestle over the gorge (narrow carriages,
  luggage loot, a one-tile ledge beside it).
- **Roads:** Route 9 (Apple Hill, Haverly, Cooper's Corner), Lake Road, River Road, the county road, the notch road,
  the logging road. Roadblocks where they leave the map. **Trails** link the rest.
- **Wilderness spots:** the fire tower on its hill, the summer camp on Pine Pond (archery: bows), the swamp, the
  quarry, the old mine, the logging camp, the island, the orchard and cider mill, the ranger station beyond the notch.
