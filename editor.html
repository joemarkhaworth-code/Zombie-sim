Painting: tap or drag with one finger. Two fingers: pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Pick a layer at the top (tap the eye to hide it), a brush and a tool at the bottom. Rect fills a rectangle; Bldg draws a building's walls, floor and roof in one go (add a door after). Height: raise and lower land; a cliff face is drawn wherever it changes. Steps join levels; a ledge lets you hop down but not climb up.
