How it works. Pick a layer at the top (the eye hides it), a brush and a tool at the bottom. One finger paints; two fingers pinch to zoom and drag to look around (or use Hand).

Paint tap or drag (size 1-3). Line a straight run: walls, fences, roads. Rect a filled rectangle. Box just its outline. Fill floods the area you tap. Bldg drag a rectangle for a whole building: walls, floor, roof, a front door facing south and windows; pick House, Cabin, Store, Diner, Barn, Garage, Mill, Church or Shed, or Demolish to clear one. Pick copies what's on a tile. Erase clears the layer.

Objects: tap to place; drag one to move it; tap a sign or a label to change its words. Turn rotates them.

Height: a cliff face is drawn wherever the ground changes level. Steps join levels; a ledge lets you hop down but not climb back up. Roofs go see-through while you work on what's under them.
