New kinds: + New makes a new ground, structure, object or roof, with its own rules (does it block walking? sight? loud underfoot?). It goes in the map editor's lists and works in the game. They travel inside the art text.

All the art, as text (to put in the game: art/tiles.json):

Pencil draws, Fill floods, Line and Rect drag out shapes, Pick copies a colour, Mirror draws both halves at once. The previews show the tile at size, tiled 3 x 3 (do the edges meet?), in grey (how it looks remembered, out of sight) and on grass. One finger draws; + and − zoom; two fingers move the picture.

Versions: Duplicate makes another version of the tile (#2, #3...) to change a little, so a field of grass or a row of fences doesn't repeat. The game picks one for each spot, the same one every time. The tiled preview mixes them.
